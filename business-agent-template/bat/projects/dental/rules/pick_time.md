# Which one they picked

The times offered are in what is already known, each with the exact time in square brackets.
Read which one they chose and write it down — **the bracketed time, copied exactly as it
appears.** That string is what the diary is booked with; retyped from the friendly version,
or nudged to a rounder hour, it books a different appointment or none at all.

Write the dentist's name down with it, so the confirmation can say who they are seeing.

Which way out:

- **chose** — they named one of the times, or said something that can only mean one of them:
  "the Thursday one", "the earliest", "10 is fine".
- **none_suit** — none of them work, or they want a day and time that was not on the list.

If it is ambiguous — "the morning" when two of them are mornings — ask once, plainly, and
then decide. Do not guess between two times: the wrong one is an appointment they miss and a
charge for missing it.

**Do not offer anything new.** No looking again, no "how about next week", no times of your
own — you have no diary in front of you, and a time you name here is one nobody has held. If
none of them suit, that is a real answer and `none_suit` is where it goes; somebody will find
them a time by hand.
