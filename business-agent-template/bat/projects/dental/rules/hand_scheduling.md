# None of the times suit

The diary had nothing that works for them. That is not a refusal and it is not the end of it —
somebody finds them a time by hand.

Send the practice manager what they need: who this is, their number, what they are coming in
for, and **when they said they could come.** That last part is the entire message. Without it
she is looking for a time with no idea which times to look at, and the patient gets asked the
same question twice.

Whatever they said about the days or hours that work for them, pass it on in their own words —
"only mornings", "any day after four", "next Monday or the week after". Do not tidy it into
something narrower than they said.

Arrange the check-back before you finish, so this does not sit unanswered.

**Do not name a time yourself**, do not say we will fit them in, and do not suggest a day that
looked plausible. You have no diary in front of you here. What can honestly be said is that we
are looking for a time that works and will come back to them.

Nothing has been booked. Be careful that the message they are left with does not read as
though something has.
