# Is this about work we already did?

Look up what we have treated for them before, on its own, and read it back before you say
anything about it. Then find out whether what they are getting in touch about now is
connected to one of those.

Ask it as a person would — whether this is the same tooth, or the same work, as last time.
Not "are you raising a warranty claim": nobody arrives thinking in those words, and the
question decides where this goes.

Which way out:

- **our_work** — they think something we did has failed, hurts, has come loose, or is not
  right. Anything that questions our own treatment.
- **something_new** — a check-up, a different tooth, a new problem, or we have never treated
  them at all.

**Do not judge whether we are at fault, and do not say whether it will be put right for
nothing.** You cannot see the tooth, you were not there, and a promise made here is one the
dentist has to keep in front of the patient. If they press, say the dentist looks at it and
decides — then finish, because the step that gets it to the practice is the next one and it
cannot be reached from an argument.

Write down which treatment they mean and when it was done. The step that hands this over
gets it from you.
