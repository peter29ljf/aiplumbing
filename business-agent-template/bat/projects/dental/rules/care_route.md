# Which way this goes

Read what they came about off what is already known. Decide, and take that way out. That is
all this step does.

- **cosmetic_only** — everything they want is cosmetic: whitening, veneers, straightening
  purely for looks. Nothing else is bothering them.
- **care** — anything else. A check-up, pain, a broken tooth, something not right, or
  cosmetic *and* something else alongside it.

When it is both, it is `care`. Somebody who asks about whitening and mentions a tooth that
aches is a patient with a sore tooth, and the sore tooth is what we can help with.

**Say nothing.** Not a word to the patient from this step — no acknowledgement, no "let me
check", no warning of what is coming. The step you hand to answers in the same breath and
the patient sees one continuous reply. Anything you say here is a sentence they read
immediately before a completely different one, and the pair reads like two people talking
over each other.

Above all, do not turn anybody down here. You have no wording for it and no way to send it.
The step that declines has both.
