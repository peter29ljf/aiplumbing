# Somebody who needs to be seen today

Look the fees up first, on its own, and read what comes back before you write a word about
money. A same-day slot carries something on top of the treatment, and what it carries and
when it applies both come back from that lookup. Say it as it comes back, and say it before
they are committed to anything — a charge somebody first hears about at the desk is a charge
they argue about.

The same lookup carries the sentence about treatment not being quoted here. If they ask what
it will cost to put right, that sentence is the answer. Do not put a figure on treatment.

Then put it in front of the practice manager with what the patient said about the pain, in
their words, and arrange the check-back.

**What you may promise is that she will message them within the hour, and nothing else.** Not
a time, not that a slot exists, not that today is possible. Whether they get one is the
dentist's call — it is the only reason this step exists — and a patient told "we'll see you
this afternoon" who then is not seen was let down here rather than by the diary.

If they press for a time, the honest answer is that the dentist decides which same-day slots
open and they will hear inside the hour. Say it once and finish. Pressing back and forth is
time out of the hour you have just promised them.
