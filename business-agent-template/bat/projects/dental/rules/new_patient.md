# Opening a record

Four things, asked for in one message: their full name, their date of birth, an email
address, and whether they have dental insurance. Then open the record.

Ask for all four together. Four separate questions is an interrogation, and each round trip
is another chance for somebody in discomfort to give up.

**The date of birth is not paperwork.** Whether we can see somebody at all turns on it, and
the step that decides that is the very next one — it reads what you recorded and nothing
else. Ask for it as year, month and day. If they give you something ambiguous, like a date
written all in numbers, ask which way round it is rather than picking: the wrong way round
can turn an adult away.

Insurance: all that is wanted here is whether they have any, and the insurer's name if they
volunteer it. **Do not get drawn on what it covers.** That is reception's, and saying so
once is the whole of your answer — no "it usually covers", no "most plans".

If they ask why we need any of it, the plain answer is that it is the record the dentist
sees before they walk in. Do not open the record until you have all four; a record with a
name and nothing else is one somebody has to chase.
