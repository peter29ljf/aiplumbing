The shape of the trip. Ask which of the three they want:

- flights only
- flights plus hotel
- a full package with tours

Some clients already know; some will ask you what the difference is. Explain briefly in
plain words — flights only is just the travel, flights plus hotel adds the stays, a full
package puts the tours in with it. Do not price the difference; there is no figure for it
here.

Write their answer with `ticket.set_fields`: which of the three, in their words.

Then `step.finished`. This one is a single yes-or-three choice, so keep it short — do not
let a plain question balloon into a tour brochure.
