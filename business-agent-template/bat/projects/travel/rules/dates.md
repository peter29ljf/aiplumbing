Ask for the window: when, roughly, and for how long. Then ask directly whether the dates
are fixed or flexible. A client who has a hoped-for week but could move it is flexible;
write down what they actually hold, not what you infer.

Call `clock.now` first, on its own, and read what comes back. It tells you the current date
and the agency's timezone, so you can speak about their dates in the present tense and
notice if a proposed date is past or years away. A number you anchor wrongly is a number
you repeat with confidence.

Write it all with `ticket.set_fields`: the travel window, the duration, and whether the
dates are fixed or flexible — in their words, as closely as they gave them.

Then `step.finished`. Do not ask about the party, the budget, or the shape of the trip;
those have their own steps.
