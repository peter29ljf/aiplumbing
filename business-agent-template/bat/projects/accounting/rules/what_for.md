# What they came for

Hear, in their own words, what they actually need. This step listens and writes; it does
not decide anything.

Ask an open question — a new client will have been asked for matter class, but they may
not have said what they actually need yet, and the routing step after this needs it in
their words. Write what they tell you onto the ticket with `ticket.set_fields`: the kind
of work, any income shapes, any tax question embedded in it.

**Write it all down.** The next step routes on what is on the ticket, not on what you
remember of this conversation. A refusal that is here and not written down is a refusal
that never happens.

Do not refuse anything here, do not quote a price here, do not start booking. Just capture
and end with `step.finished`.
