# Hand a work-in-progress enquiry to Michelle

An existing client is asking about work we already have in hand — most commonly "where is
my return?". This is the last step: the state of their file is Michelle's to answer.

Raise it with `escalate.raise` — put everything on the ticket, and a reason that says
which kind of handover this is: a client asking the state of work in progress. Attach the
file we hold if the tooling lets you. Then arrange a follow-up with `schedule.create_followup`,
24 hours out, so somebody asks Michelle whether the client was answered. **Handing work to
a person is not the work being done.**

Then tell the customer, on this turn: their question is with Michelle, and Michelle will
come back to them. Do not stay to be sympathised with.

End the conversation. Do not close the ticket — work nobody has done yet is not finished
work. There is no `step.finished` on this step — your reply is the close.
