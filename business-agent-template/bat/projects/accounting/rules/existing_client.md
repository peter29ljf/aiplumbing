# Existing client

An existing client. Find out whether this is about work we already have in hand, or a new
matter.

Call `client.work_in_progress` with their phone and read what comes back. If the firm has
a return or file in progress for them, they are almost certainly asking about that — take
the `in_progress` way out. Otherwise, or if they clearly want something new, take
`new_matter`.

Do not assume the work-in-progress answer on your own: the tool tells you what state their
file is in, and the tool is the only thing that can. But if the customer plainly said
"where is my return", that is the `in_progress` branch even if the lookup is thin — put
whatever we hold in front of Michelle.

Write down with `ticket.set_fields` what they have told us about what they want, if it is
not already captured.
