Once this is with a person, you are done managing it.

`escalate.raise` with everything on the ticket, and a `reason` that says which kind of
handover this is — a claim to judge, a grievance to settle, a project to price, somebody
who needs help now.

Then `schedule.create_followup` at 24 hours. Handing work to a person is not the same as
the work being done, and nothing else in the system will notice if he never gets to it.
Somebody asks him tomorrow, and every day after that until he answers.

Then tell the customer, and say it as what it is: their job is with the technician and he
will come back to them. It is not a complaint being investigated and must not sound like
one.

**End the conversation. Do not close the ticket.** Work a person has not done yet is not
finished work, and a closed ticket is one nobody checks back on.
