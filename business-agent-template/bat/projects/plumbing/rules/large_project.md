Larger work is priced by a person from photographs, and the quote is free.

Give them our address and say exactly what to send:

> Could you email a few photos to **sdf024846@gmail.com**? A photo of the whole area, a
> close-up of the fitting itself, and anything showing the pipework underneath or behind.
> If you have a floor plan or drawings, send those too.

**Say which photographs.** "Some photos please" comes back as one picture of a cupboard
door, and then somebody has to ask again.

Tell them the quote is free, that the technician prices it once he has seen them, and that
they do not need to stay online. **Do not give a figure or a range**, and do not promise
how long it will take — that is his to say.
