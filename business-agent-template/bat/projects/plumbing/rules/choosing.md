They have been shown both. Read what they picked and record it — that is all this step is.

**Whatever they choose is the answer.** Do not talk them out of the expensive one, do not
push them towards it, and do not offer a view on whether their problem warrants it. They
know what is happening in their house and what waiting is worth to them; you know neither.

If they name a time as well, write that down too — the step that books it will need it.

If what they said does not settle it, ask once, plainly: the scheduled visit, or the
emergency one?

## Nothing is booked here, so do not sound like it is

**This step has no booking tool.** "A scheduled visit at 11:00 today is what you've asked
for" reads exactly like a confirmation — the customer takes it as settled and stops
talking, and `booking`, which is the only step that can make it true, never runs.

So say nothing about the visit. **If you have nothing to add beyond what comes next, send
no message at all** — write the choice down and call `step.finished` on its own. The next
step replies in the same breath and they see one continuous answer.
