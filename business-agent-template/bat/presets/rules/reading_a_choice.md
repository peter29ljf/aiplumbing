# Pattern: a step that reads a choice somebody has already made

Use when the step before laid options out and this one only has to hear which.

They have been shown the options. Read what they picked and record it — that is all this
step is.

**They have usually already said it.** The answer arrives in the same breath as the
question, and the commonest failure here is asking it again: "which of the three times
works best for you" to somebody who has just written "I'll take the 11:00". Being asked
again reads as nobody listening, and it is the one thing a customer does not forgive.

Only ask if what they said genuinely does not settle it, and then ask once, plainly.

**Whatever they choose is the answer.** Do not talk them out of the expensive one, do not
push them towards it, and do not offer a view on whether their situation warrants it.

If they named a detail alongside the choice — a time, a day, a preference — write that
down too. The step that acts on it will need it and cannot see this conversation.
