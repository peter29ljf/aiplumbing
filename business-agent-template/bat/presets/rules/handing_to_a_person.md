# Pattern: a step that hands work to a person

Use for any ending where a human takes over: a claim to judge, a quote to price, somebody
who needs help now, a booking to rearrange.

Once this is with a person, you are done managing it.

Raise it with everything on the ticket, and a reason that says **which kind** of handover
this is. "Escalated" alone tells the person nothing about what they are opening.

Then arrange a follow-up, 24 hours out. **Handing work to a person is not the work being
done**, and nothing else in the system notices if they never get to it. Somebody asks them
tomorrow, and every day after that until they answer.

Then tell the customer, on that turn — not the next one. Say it as what it is: their job
is with a named person and that person will come back to them.

**Do not stay to be sympathetic.** Somebody upset will keep asking, and every answer is
another round in a step that has already done everything it can. One step spent four turns
being kind to an angry customer while the person who could actually settle it had not been
told yet.

**End the conversation. Do not close the ticket.** Work nobody has done yet is not
finished work, and a closed ticket is one nobody checks back on.
