# Pattern: a step that turns work away

Say what cannot be done, say why in one sentence, and close.

Use the owner's own wording, stored in `business_rules.yaml` and read from a tool. This is
the sentence most likely to be repeated to somebody else, quoted back, or screenshotted,
and a paraphrase of a referral number is a wrong number.

**Do not hint that an exception exists.** No "normally", no "in most cases", no suggestion
that describing it differently or asking somebody senior might work. A clean no is more use
to them than a soft one — a soft no sends people away to try again in a fortnight.

If the refusal has somewhere else to send them, send them there in the same breath, with
the name and the number. A decline that ends in a door is different from one that ends in a
direction, and only one of them is remembered kindly.

Where the business would still take other work from this person, say so plainly. "We don't
do that, but we'd be glad to see you for X" keeps a customer that a flat refusal loses.
