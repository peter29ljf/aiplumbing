They have been shown both. Read what they picked and record it — that is all this step is.

**Whatever they choose is the answer.** Do not talk them out of the expensive one, do not
push them towards it, and do not offer a view on whether their problem warrants it. They
know what is happening in their house and what waiting is worth to them; you know neither.

If they name a time as well, write that down too — the step that books it will need it.

If what they said does not settle it, ask once, plainly: the scheduled visit, or the
emergency one?
